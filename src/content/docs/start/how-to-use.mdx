Epiglossary is a map of Epicor built from real support and development work. It won't replace Epicor's documentation or the community forum. It's meant to get you to the right idea quickly, then point you at the places with more depth.

## Find an answer in four moves

<Steps>

1. **Search first.** Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>/</kbd>) anywhere. Search works best with the words Epicor itself uses:
   - the **exact error text** from the message box
   - a **business object or method**, such as `SalesOrder.Update` or `GetNewCustomer`
   - a **table or field**, such as `PartTran`, `TranType` or `OrderRel`
   - a **screen name**, such as `Customer Shipment Entry`

2. **Check the badge.** Every article says which client it applies to:

   | Badge | Meaning |
   |---|---|
   | **Kinetic** | The browser client and Application Studio |
   | **Classic (E10)** | The Epicor 10 smart client, including customizations, `oTrans` and Event Wizard |
   | **Kinetic + Classic** | Server-side or functional topics that behave the same in both, such as BPMs, Functions, BAQs, SSRS and the business processes |

   If you're on Kinetic and land on a Classic page, look for the matching Kinetic page. [Migrating to Kinetic](/classic/migrating-to-kinetic/) maps each Classic technique to its Kinetic replacement.

3. **Start at the overview.** Every area (BPM, BAQ, Scheduling and so on) opens with an **Overview** page that explains the concepts and links everything in the area. When you're new to an area, read that page before a how-to.

4. **Use the troubleshooting page.** Most areas end with a **Troubleshooting** page in symptom → cause → fix form. Error messages are quoted exactly, so searching for the text of your error usually lands there.

</Steps>

## How to read a page

- **Placeholders.** Examples use made-up values so they're safe to share: company `EPIC06`, custom prefix `XX_`, parts like `PART-1001`, and emails at `example.com`. Swap in your own values, and use your company's naming convention for anything you create.
- **Test in a non-production environment first.** Every BPM, function and data change here should be tried in Pilot or Test before Live.
- **Sources.** When a page was informed by a public thread or article, the **Sources** list at the bottom links to it. The original discussion often has more context, alternatives and follow-ups.
- **Version notes.** Most pages hold across versions. Where behavior changed in a release, the page says so inline.

## Where to go when Epiglossary doesn't have it

<Steps>

1. **Search EpiUsers.** Someone has very likely hit the same thing. Search for the error text or method name, and filter to the **Kinetic ERP** or **Epicor ERP 10** category.
2. **Check Epicor's own help.** Press **F1** in Kinetic for field and screen help, or open the **Help & Support** panel. Customers can also search the **EpicCare** knowledge base and problem repository (PRBs) to see whether it's a known bug.
3. **Trace it.** If you need to know *what Epicor is doing*, capture a trace and read the business object calls. See [Find the method a screen calls](/platform/bpm/finding-the-right-method/) and [Debugging](/kinetic/application-studio/debugging/).
4. **Ask well.** When you post, describe the goal rather than only the fix you tried ([the XY problem](https://xyproblem.info/)). Include your version, the exact error and what you've already checked.

</Steps>
